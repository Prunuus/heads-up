## The exploit modes

At most one mode applies per decision, by detector precedence check-folder > maniac >
station. I6 applies on top of any mode. Every trigger is the opponent model's current
evidence (`bot/opponent.py`), re-read at every decision.

| Mode | Trigger (bot/opponent.py) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| check-folder (I2) | its last 12 bet-facing decisions were folds, and it has not bet or raised since the first of them | RAISE_HALF whenever legal, else the base |
| maniac | bet/raise rate > 0.55 over its last 200 decisions where a raise was legal (n >= 80) | the heuristic re-run with call_margin and bet_range 0.06 lower, range_weight capped at 0.5, no bluffs |
| station | postflop fold-to-bet < 0.05 over its last 150 bet-facing postflop decisions (n >= 60) | the heuristic re-run with value_raise and thin_value 0.08 lower, no bluffs |
| I6 | river, facing a bet of >= 0.4 x the pot before it, the weakest category it showed down after betting/raising the river (>= 5 showdowns) is above our made category (`I6_CATEGORY_MARGIN = 1`; the plan's literal ">=" is margin 0, see below), and we lack the nuts | FOLD instead of CHECK_CALL |

Guards: the re-run modes never fold a hand the base plays on. No mode draws from the
shared rng, so a decision the layer leaves alone is v1's, and so is the rest of the Monte
Carlo stream. With no Features (a blueprint base, Task 20), only check-folder and I6 apply.

## Activation and false positives

The rates are in the two tables above.

- **check-folder.** It fires against checkfold_after_300 after hand 300, and against
  mc_equity_slow, mc_equity_fast and llm_simple_rules. Those three ran out of their 8 s
  bank in 6/6 halves (the sim's bank_exhausted flag), so this is I2 working as H1
  predicts. Its one false positive is nit, at 1.6-1.7% of decisions. That is the T13
  ruling's harmless case: betting into a nit is +EV, and the mode reverts on its first
  call. v2 - v1 there is -0.017 [-0.075, +0.041].
- **maniac.** It fires against maniac, always_half and always_pot (97%), random (80%)
  and llm_aggressive (HOLDOUT, 97%). Each bets or raises more than 55% of the time it can.
- **station.** It fires against calling_station only (94%).
- **No mode.** None fires against guide_example; the LLM bots beginner_2h, gto_inspired,
  mc_equity, ml_minded, opp_model and time_careful; or threshold_cat. That includes both
  adaptive bots (llm_ml_minded on TRAIN, llm_opp_model on HOLDOUT). Against these bots,
  v2 differs from v1 only through I6, which made 0-2 river folds per bot.
- **The remaining spread is timing noise.** With no mode on, v2's decision is v1's for
  the same Features (unit test `test_no_evidence_means_the_base_and_no_rng_draws`). But
  the time manager sizes Monte Carlo from the wall-clock pace, so under CPU contention the
  two versions draw different rollout counts. Examples:
  - llm_opp_model: -0.18 [-0.40, +0.04] here, with no mode and no I6 fold. The first run
    gave +0.16 [-0.05, +0.37], with 3 margin-0 I6 folds;
  - llm_ml_minded, a thin-margin bot where no mode fired: v1 chopped 3 times, v2 won 3 times.

Against adaptive bots: every trigger is the detectors' current evidence. Check-folder
stops at the opponent's first call, bet or raise (`test_checkfolder_mode_stops_when_the_opponent_fights_back`:
a bot that check-folds 100 hands and then raises every hand is read as a check-folder
until its first raise shows, and as a maniac later). Station and maniac read
150/200-decision windows, so they revert as fast late in a half as early. No mode adds a
link between bet size and hand strength.

### I6: why the comparison is strict

The plan's literal rule folds when shown >= ours. On TRAIN bots (llm_beginner_2h,
guide_example, nit, llm_mc_equity, llm_gto_inspired, mc_equity_fast; 3 seeds), every fold
it made was a same-category spot. A counterfactual check against the known deal gave 11
folds: calling would have won 6 and lost 5, at 25-33% pot odds, -292 chips in all. With
`I6_CATEGORY_MARGIN = 1`, I6 folds only below every category it has shown, and it fires
0-2 times per bot in these runs. Setting the margin to 0 restores the literal rule.

On HOLDOUT, margin 0 had made 18 folds vs threshold_cat, at -0.009 [-0.043, +0.024]. That
run was seen before the TRAIN diagnostic, and it did not inform the decision.

## Reproduce

From the worktree root:

    taskset -c 0-7 nice -n 10 .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_exploit.py tests/test_policy.py
    taskset -c 0-7 nice -n 10 .venv/bin/python -m pytest -m slow -q -s tests/test_exploit.py

The per-opponent tables come from scratch runners that are not committed; their paths are
in the Task 14 report.
- For every (opponent, seed), v1 and v2 play the same duplicate match side by side on 2
  workers, and v2's `memory["exploits"]` counters are kept per half.
- The CI is paired_diff's: per-deal bb of v2 minus v1, all deals pooled,
  mean +- 1.96 sd / sqrt(N).
- Pairs must come from one run. The time manager is wall-clock paced, so a v2 re-run
  under different contention is not paired with an older v1 run.
